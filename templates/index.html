SIMPLE 3-STEP WORKFLOW

How It Works

From camera setup to instant cloud reports in three straightforward steps.

STEP 01

Create Subject & Share QR

Teachers create subjects in seconds and share join codes or QR codes for seamless student registration and enrollment into class rosters.

STEP 02

Dual AI Recognition

Teachers take a classroom photo or record an audio clip. The machine learning pipeline extracts embeddings and matches registered student profiles in milliseconds.

STEP 03

Real-Time Cloud Ledger

Attendance is committed to Supabase immediately. Students can see their attendance status, and teachers can export complete Excel/CSV summaries with timestamps.
